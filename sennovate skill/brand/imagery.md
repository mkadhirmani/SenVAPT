# Sennovate Imagery & Visual Style

**Authority:** `[OFFICIAL BRAND RULE]` for Brand Photography & Mockups; `[PRODUCT UI STANDARD]` for UI Illustration & Avatars.  
**Source:** Sennovate Brand Guidelines (Slides 42–50)

---

## 1. Visual Direction & Photography `[OFFICIAL BRAND RULE]`

Sennovate's visual aesthetic communicates **Enterprise Reliability, Human Accessibility, and Architectural Rigor**.

### Preferred Visual Attributes:
* **Subject Matter:**
  * Clean, bright, modern enterprise environments (offices, collaborative teams, secure data operations).
  * Human-centric technology: Real professionals working with intuitive tools, not distressed users or shadowy hackers in hoodies.
* **Lighting & Composition:**
  * Natural light, airy compositions with generous negative space.
  * Subtle blue atmospheric lighting or clean neutral architecture.
  * Minimalist, uncluttered backgrounds allowing UI or typography to stand out.
* **3D & Abstract Elements:**
  * Clean, glass-and-metal or matte 3D security emblems (shields, padlocks, identity nodes, connected clouds).
  * Subtle monochromatic gradients using Sennovate Blue (`#006FE3`) and official tints.

---

## 2. UI Illustrations & Empty States `[PRODUCT UI STANDARD]`

* When an illustration is needed for an empty state (e.g. "No audit logs found", "No API keys created"):
  * Use minimalist vector linework with subtle fills.
  * Primary linework in Sennovate Dark Blue (`#001B41`) or Slate Gray (`#4A5568`).
  * Accent highlights in 10%–30% blue tint and Sennovate Blue (`#006FE3`).
  * Avoid colorful cartoonish characters or 3D clay avatars.

---

## 3. User Avatars `[PRODUCT UI STANDARD]`

* **Default / Fallback Avatar:**
  * Circular container (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  * Background: Soft blue tint or Sennovate Dark Blue (`#001B41`).
  * Text: User initials in Satoshi Bold (e.g., `SP` for Senthil Palaniappan).
  * Color: Sennovate Blue (`#006FE3`) on light, Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on dark.

---

## 4. Imagery Things to Avoid (Agent Prohibitions)

* ❌ **No "Matrix" Green Code:** Avoid dated "green falling code" or stereotypical hooded cyber-criminal imagery.
* ❌ **No Heavy Neon Cyberpunk:** Avoid harsh magenta/neon-green cyberpunk visuals.
* ❌ **No Cheesy Stock Photos:** Avoid exaggerated handshake or pointing-at-screen stock photography.
* ❌ **No Low-Resolution or Stretched Graphics:** Always preserve native aspect ratios.
